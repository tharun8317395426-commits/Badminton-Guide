Rules-Court Dimensions

Essential BWF scoring rules, service guidelines, and court measurements.

The Rally Point System

Under the standard Badminton World Federation (BWF) rules, matches are played using the rally point system. A point is scored on every single rally, regardless of who served.

  • A match consists of the best of 3 games played to 21 points each.
  • The side winning a rally adds one point to their current score.
  • At 20-all, the side which gains a 2-point lead first wins that game (e.g. 22-20, 24-22).
  • At 29-all, the side scoring the 30th point wins the game outright (maximum cap is 30).
  • The side winning a game serves first in the subsequent game.

Serving Guidelines

Serving starts every rally and follows strict court positioning:

  • When the server's score is an even number (0, 2, 4...), the server serves from the right service court diagonally to the receiver's right service court.
  • When the server's score is an odd number (1, 3, 5...), the server serves from the left service court diagonally to the receiver's left service court.
  • The entire shuttlecock must be below 1.15 meters (or waist height) at the instant of being hit by the server's racket.
  • Both the server and receiver must remain stationary within their respective service boxes until the serve is struck.

Common Match Faults

  • Touching the net with the racket, body, or clothing during a live rally.
  • Hitting the shuttlecock twice consecutively by the same player or side.
  • Reaching over the net to strike the shuttle before it crosses the net plane.
  • The shuttle landing outside the boundary lines, passing through or under the net.
  • Distracting an opponent through shouting or hand waving during their stroke.
